# Telecaller guide

## Signing in
Use your email and password. The first time (temporary password) you must choose your own: at least 10 characters with upper case, lower case and a number.

## Your day
1. **Attendance → Clock in** (Start/End break when needed; Clock out at the end). Late means after your shift start plus the grace time.
2. **My Workspace:** your call target progress, today's follow-ups and the numbers for your leads. Press **Start next lead**.
3. The lead screen shows the customer, previous call notes and promised follow-up. Press **Start call**:
   - If your company uses system calling, your phone rings first, then the customer is connected.
   - Otherwise your phone's dialler opens with the number.
4. After the call choose the **outcome** (press its number key), type notes (N), schedule a **follow-up** if needed (F; required for "Callback requested"), then **Save activity** (Ctrl/⌘ + Enter).
5. Press **Next eligible lead**. Leads you called recently or whose follow-up isn't due yet won't appear yet.
6. **Follow-ups** page: mark done or reschedule. The **bell** shows alerts.
7. **My performance** shows your numbers, targets, incentive statements and feedback on reviewed calls.

## Good to know
- What you type is kept on your device until saved, so a refresh or lost connection won't lose it; pressing Save twice never creates a second call.
- Do-Not-Disturb customers cannot be called; ask your manager.
- You only see your own leads. If a link says "not found", it isn't assigned to you.
- If something looks wrong, tell your team leader; don't share your password with anyone.
